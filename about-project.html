About Intelligence Map

Intelligence Map is Bohundefence's research-oriented atlas of publicly documented defence and dual-use industries. It connects companies, sectors and primary sources to build a clearer view of the industrial ecosystem.

Current data coverage is partial and evolving. The map is not a list of Bohundefence clients, customers or partners. Information should be checked against its original source before relying on it.

Analytical news signals and experimental functions remain unpublished until the evidence and editorial gates are met.
