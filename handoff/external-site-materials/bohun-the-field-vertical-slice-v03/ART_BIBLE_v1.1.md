# BOHUN / THE FIELD — Art Bible v1.1

## Direction
Original late-1990s / early-2000s isometric RTS grammar with a recognisable Ukrainian commercial geography. The target is map richness, density and strategic readability, not imitation of any specific Cossacks asset, UI, map or trade dress.

## World
- Ukraine is the primary continuous territory.
- Europe sits spatially west of the border, not in a separate card.
- Black Sea, river, border corridors and roads create macro geography.
- World density should come from settlements, factories, warehouses, forests, roads and ambient movement.

## Camera
- Fixed isometric/pseudo-isometric presentation around 30–40° perceived elevation.
- Presentation view should read the country/route structure at a glance.
- Free camera is reserved for Explore The Field.

## Materials
- terrain greens and farmland ochres;
- muted water blue/grey;
- warm stone, timber and industrial steel;
- Bohun gold for meaningful action/state only.

## Structures
Bohun HQ, supplier factory, partner compound, warehouse, communications, industrial site, settlement structures, port/logistics marker.

## Gameplay states
- dormant
- selected
- researched
- revealed
- liaison en route
- route available
- connected

## UI
Minimap + selected-object panel + command actions. Modern semantic HTML for substantive text. No health bars, crosshairs, resource counters, fake classified decoration or weapon-selection grammar.
